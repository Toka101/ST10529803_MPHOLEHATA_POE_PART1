About Nova Android

Nova Android was founded in 2019 in Johannesburg with a clear purpose: to make premium mobile technology more accessible to South African consumers. Our founding team brought together expertise in electronics, product design, and customer experience, with the goal of building smartphones that blend reliability, innovation, and value.

From our first three smartphone models, we have expanded to a broad portfolio that serves students, professionals, creators, and families. Today, Nova Android is recognised as one of the country’s most promising local Android brands, with a growing reputation for practical, performance-driven devices.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To deliver reliable, innovative, and affordable Android smartphones that help South Africans stay connected, productive, and inspired in everyday life.

Our vision

To become Africa’s most trusted Android brand by putting customer value, product quality, and sustainable innovation at the heart of everything we do.

Our promise

We aim to combine cutting-edge features with practical usability so each device serves real needs without unnecessary complexity.
