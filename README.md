1.1	 Brief History 

  

Nova Android was established in 2019 in Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa. Founded by a team of tech engineers with a vision to bring flagship-level technology and affordable innovation together, the company began with 3 smartphone models focused on battery life and camera performance. 

  

Over the years, Nova Android expanded its product line to 12 devices, including budget, mid-range, and flagship phones, gaining a reputation as one of South Africa’s fastest-growing local Android brands. 

  

By 2024, Nova Android had earned multiple tech awards, including Best Local Smartphone Brand in South Africa. The company has launched 2 major product refresh cycles — in 2021 and 2023 — to modernise its hardware and software while preserving its commitment to clean Android, fast charging, and value for money. Nova Android is a proud partner of Google’s Android One program.
